Einstein's photoelectric equation
\\(E_\\gamma=h\\nu=\\dfrac{hc}{\\lambda}=\\phi+K_{\\max}\\)
\\(K_{\\max}=\\max(E_\\gamma-\\phi,0)\\)
\\(\\lambda_{dB}=\\dfrac{h}{p},\\quad p=\\sqrt{2m_eK_{\\max}}\\)

The wavelength control changes photon frequency and energy. Intensity changes the photon arrival rate. At a fixed wavelength and metal, increasing intensity increases the number of emitted electrons, not their maximum kinetic energy.
