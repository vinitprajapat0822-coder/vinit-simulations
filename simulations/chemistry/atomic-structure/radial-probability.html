How to read the graph

R(r) can be positive or negative; its sign changes at a radial node.

R²(r) is the local radial wavefunction amplitude squared.

4πr²R² includes the spherical-shell factor, so its peaks show the most probable radial distances.

Dashed vertical markers identify radial nodes where the radial wavefunction crosses zero. The origin at r = 0 is not counted as a radial node.
