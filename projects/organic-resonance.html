Deep theory · exceptions · JEE traps

Hard rules

  1. Same number of electrons / same net charge in every form.
  2. Same atom connectivity — resonance is not isomerisation.
  3. Full octets preferred for second-row atoms when possible.
  4. Charge separation usually raises energy (minor form).

JEE traps

Writing arrows that move atoms, or “breaking” C–C σ frames for ordinary resonance, is a common exam error.

Major vs minor

More covalent bonds, less charge separation, negative charge on more EN atoms → usually major contributor.
