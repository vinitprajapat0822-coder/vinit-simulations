Angular Acceleration Laboratory

Constant angular acceleration: ω = ω₀ + αt and θ = ω₀t + ½αt² — the rotational SUVAT analogues.

Controls
What to notice
Linear and angular variables link by r: s = rθ, v = rω, a_t = rα (when r fixed).
  • Same algebraic structure as 1D kinematics with constant a.
  • Use rad for θ in formulas (2π rad = 1 revolution).

Constant α

ω = ω₀ + αt
θ = ω₀t + ½αt²
ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ
