ROLLING

Pure Rolling Laboratory

Pure rolling means v = ωr — the point of contact is instantaneously at rest relative to the ground.

Controls
What to notice
If v ≠ ωr there is slipping. Kinetic friction then adjusts ω and v until pure rolling begins (on a rough surface).
  • KE = ½mv² + ½Iω² for rolling.
  • For a disc I = ½mr² → KE = ¾mv².
