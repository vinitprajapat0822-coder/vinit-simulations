PRESSURE

Hydrostatic Pressure Laboratory

Pressure in a liquid increases with depth: P = ρgh (gauge pressure). Independent of container shape.

Controls
What to notice
At the same depth, pressure is the same in connected fluid (Pascal) — basis of hydraulic machines.
  • Double depth → double gauge pressure.
  • Atmospheric pressure adds for absolute pressure.

Theory & formulas

Hydrostatic gauge pressure

P = ρ g h

Absolute

P_abs = P_atm + ρgh

Problem tip

Use consistent units: ρ in kg/m³, h in m → P in pascals.
