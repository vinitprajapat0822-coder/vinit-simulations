REFRACTION

Snell's Law Laboratory

Refraction at a plane interface: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r. When light goes denser→rarer beyond critical angle, TIR occurs.

Controls
What to notice
Going into higher n bends the ray toward the normal. Try n₁ > n₂ and large i to see TIR (red reflected ray).
