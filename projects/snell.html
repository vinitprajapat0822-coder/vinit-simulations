What to notice
When light goes into a denser medium (n₂ > n₁), it bends toward the normal. Into rarer medium, it bends away — and may undergo TIR.
  • Critical angle exists only when n₁ > n₂.
  • sin c = n₂/n₁.

Theory & formulas

Snell’s law

n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂

Critical angle (n₁ > n₂)

sin c = n₂ / n₁

Problem tip

Always measure angles from the normal, not from the surface.
