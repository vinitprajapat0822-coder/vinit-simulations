What to notice
New Cartesian sign convention: direction of incident light is positive. For a concave mirror on the left of the object, f is negative in some books — this lab uses the common school form with f > 0 for concave and u < 0.
  • |u| > 2f → real, inverted, diminished.
  • |u| < f → virtual, erect, magnified (makeup mirror).
