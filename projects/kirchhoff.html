KIRCHHOFF

Kirchhoff Loop Laboratory

Single-loop circuit: loop rule ΣV = 0. Current I = E/(R1+R2); voltages divide in proportion to resistance.

Controls
What to notice
Going around the loop: +E when you leave the negative terminal through the battery, −IR across each resistor in the direction of assumed current.
  • Larger R gets larger share of the voltage.
  • ΣV should be ~0 (numerical check in results).

Theory & formulas

Loop rule

ΣV = 0 around a closed loop

Series resistors

I = E / (R₁+R₂)
V₁ = I R₁ · V₂ = I R₂

Problem tip

Assign a current direction first; if the answer is negative, the true direction is opposite.
