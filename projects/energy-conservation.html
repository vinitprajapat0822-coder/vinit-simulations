Mechanical Energy Conservation

A mass falls from height h. Potential energy converts to kinetic energy; total mechanical energy stays constant (no air resistance).

Controls
What to notice
At the top: all PE. At the bottom: all KE. In between they trade, sum fixed.
  • v = √(2gh) from rest, independent of mass.
  • Air resistance would drain mechanical energy to heat.

Theory & formulas

Conservation

mgh + ½mv² = constant (ideal)

From rest at height h

½mv² = mgh ⇒ v = √(2gh)

Problem tip

Set PE = 0 at the lowest point of interest to simplify algebra.
