Young's Double-Slit Laboratory

Two coherent slits produce bright and dark fringes on a screen. Fringe width β = λD/d — the standard YDSE result.

Controls
What to notice
Path difference at angle θ is d sinθ. Bright fringes when path difference is mλ.
  • Larger λ → wider fringes.
  • Larger d → narrower fringes.
  • Larger D → wider pattern on the screen.

Theory & formulas

Fringe width

β = λD / d

Position of m-th bright fringe

y_m = m λD / d (small angle)

Problem tip

Convert nm and mm carefully to metres before substituting into β = λD/d.
