YDSE

Young's Double-Slit Laboratory

Two coherent slits produce an interference pattern on a distant screen. Fringe width β = λD/d. Bright fringes where path difference is mλ.

Controls
What to notice
Narrower d or larger D spreads the fringes. Longer λ (redder light) also widens β.
  • Central fringe is bright for usual monochromatic YDSE.
  • Intensity follows cos² of the phase difference.
