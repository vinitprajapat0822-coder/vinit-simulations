TORQUE

Torque Laboratory

Torque τ = rF sinθ measures the turning effect of a force about a pivot. Maximum when force is perpendicular to the position vector.

Controls
What to notice
Only the component of F perpendicular to r contributes. If θ = 0, force is along the line to the pivot → no turning.
  • τ = 0 when sinθ = 0.
  • Larger lever arm → larger torque for same F.

Theory & formulas

Torque

τ = r F sinθ
τ = r_⊥ F · r_⊥ = r sinθ

Problem tip

Choose the pivot wisely so unknown forces produce zero torque when possible.
