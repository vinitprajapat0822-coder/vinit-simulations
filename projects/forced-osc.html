FORCED OSCILLATIONS

Forced Oscillations Laboratory

A driven oscillator settles into motion at the drive frequency. Amplitude is largest near ω₀ (see also the Resonance lab).

Controls
What to notice
After transients die, the system oscillates at ω_drive, not ω₀ — but the size of the motion depends on how close those frequencies are.
  • Off-resonance: small motion.
  • On-resonance: large motion (damping limits the peak).
