FREE FALL

Free Fall Laboratory

Drop or toss a ball from height h. Gravity alone sets the acceleration. Optional upward launch velocity delays the impact and raises the peak.

Controls
What to notice
At the highest point velocity is zero for an instant; acceleration is still g downward. Impact speed depends on drop height and launch speed through energy or SUVAT.
  • Purple inset: velocity vs time is a straight line with slope −g.
  • Same impact speed from height h whether dropped or thrown horizontally (vertical part only matters).

Equations (up positive)

y = h + u t − ½ g t²
v = u − g t
v² = u² − 2g(y−h) careful with signs

From rest at height h

T = √(2h/g) · v_impact = √(2gh)

Problem tip

Choose a sign convention once; stick to it for every term.
