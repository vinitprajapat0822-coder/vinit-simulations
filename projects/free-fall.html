Free Fall Laboratory

Drop or throw a body straight down from height h. See how time of fall and impact speed depend on h, g, and initial downward speed.

What to notice
Acceleration is constant g downward. Velocity grows linearly; distance grows as t² if u₀=0.
  • From rest: v² = 2gh at the ground.
  • Same height on Moon (smaller g) → longer fall time, smaller impact speed.

Theory & formulas

Equations (down positive)

v = u₀ + g t
y = u₀ t + ½ g t²
v² = u₀² + 2 g y

Time to fall from height h

T = [−u₀ + √(u₀² + 2gh)] / g

Use in problems

  • Set u₀=0 for pure free fall from rest.
  • Change g to 1.6 to mimic Moon and compare T and v.
