RELATIVE VELOCITY

River–Boat Laboratory

A boat crosses a flowing river. Compare heading straight across (with drift) versus aiming upstream to go straight to the opposite bank — classic relative-velocity problems.

Controls
What to notice
Velocity of boat relative to ground = velocity of boat in water + vector velocity of water relative to ground.
  • Perpendicular heading: shortest heading, but longer path and drift.
  • Straight across: longer time if vb > vr; impossible if vb ≤ vr.
Live results
Theory & formulas

Head perpendicular to banks

t = width / vb
drift = vr · t

Straight across (heading upstream)

vacross = √(vb² − vr²)
t = width / vacross · drift = 0
