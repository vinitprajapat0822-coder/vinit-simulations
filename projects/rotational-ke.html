ROTATIONAL KE

Rotational Kinetic Energy

A rotating rigid body stores energy KE = ½Iω². Larger I or ω means more energy at the same spin rate.

Controls
What to notice
Rolling body has both translational and rotational KE: ½mv² + ½Iω².
  • Double ω → 4× rotational KE.
  • Flywheels store energy via large I and high ω.
