Viscosity (Stokes) Laboratory

Stokes’ law: drag on a small sphere F = 6πηrv. Terminal velocity problems use this with buoyancy and weight.

Controls
What to notice
At terminal speed, weight = buoyancy + drag. Solving gives v_t ∝ r² for a sphere in viscous fluid.
  • Higher η → more drag → lower terminal speed.
  • Larger r → more drag, but weight grows faster (∝ r³).
