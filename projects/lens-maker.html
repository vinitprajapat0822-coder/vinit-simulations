Focal length from refractive index and the two radii of curvature: 1/f = (n−1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂).

Controls
What to notice
Sign convention for radii matters. For a double-convex lens in air, R₁ > 0 and R₂ < 0 in the common convention used here.
  • Larger |R| → weaker surface → longer |f|.
  • Higher n → stronger lens (shorter f).
