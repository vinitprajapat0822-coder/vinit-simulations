POLARIZATION

Polarization — Malus's Law

Intensity after an analyzer is I = I₀ cos²θ, where θ is the angle between polarizer and analyzer axes.

Controls
What to notice
At θ = 90° the analyzer blocks all light (ideal case). At θ = 0° transmission is maximum.
  • Two crossed polarizers → dark.
  • Inserting a third polarizer in between can let some light through.
