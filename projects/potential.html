Electric Potential Laboratory

Potential of a point charge V = kq/r (zero at infinity). Equipotentials are spheres centered on the charge.

What to notice
Potential is a scalar — easier to superpose than E. ΔV relates to work: W = qΔV.
  • Near a positive charge, V is high.
  • E points from high V to low V.

Problem tip

Never confuse V (volts) with E (N/C or V/m).
