CENTRE OF MASS

Centre of Mass Laboratory

For two particles on a line, x_cm = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂)/(m₁+m₂). The CM lies closer to the heavier mass.

Controls
What to notice
External forces accelerate the CM as if all mass were concentrated there: F_ext = M a_cm.
  • Equal masses → CM at the midpoint.
  • If m₂ ≫ m₁, CM ≈ x₂.

Problem tip
