SIMPLE PENDULUM

Simple Pendulum Laboratory

A mass on a light string swings with small amplitude. Period depends on length and g — not on mass. Match the SHM approximation used in class.

Controls
What to notice
For small angles (θ₀ ≲ 15°), motion is approximately SHM with T = 2π √(L/g).
  • Doubling L multiplies T by √2.
  • Mass does not appear in T.
  • On Moon (g≈1.6) the same pendulum is slower.

Theory & formulas

Small-angle model

θ = θ₀ cos(ωt) with ω = √(g/L)
T = 2π √(L/g)

Energy idea

At ends: mostly gravitational PE. At bottom: mostly KE.

Problem tip

If a question gives L and asks T, use the formula above. This lab shows the swing and the number side by side.
