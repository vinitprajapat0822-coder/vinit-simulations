Collision & Momentum Laboratory

1D collisions with adjustable masses and restitution e. Watch total momentum stay constant while KE drops unless e = 1.

Controls
What to notice
Equal masses, e = 1: velocities exchange. e = 0: they move together after impact (perfectly inelastic if they stick — here modelled via e).
  • Total p is conserved (no external impulse).
  • KE is conserved only for e = 1.
