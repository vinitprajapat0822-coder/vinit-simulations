What to notice
Momentum is conserved in both cases (no external force along the line). Kinetic energy is conserved only for the ideal elastic case.
  • Equal masses, elastic, target at rest → velocities exchange.
  • Inelastic → they stick and move with common velocity.

Theory & formulas

Momentum

m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂

Elastic (1D)

v₁ = ((m₁−m₂)/(m₁+m₂))u₁ + (2m₂/(m₁+m₂))u₂
v₂ = (2m₁/(m₁+m₂))u₁ + ((m₂−m₁)/(m₁+m₂))u₂

Perfectly inelastic

v = (m₁u₁ + m₂u₂)/(m₁+m₂)
