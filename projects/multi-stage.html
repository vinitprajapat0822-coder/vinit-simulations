Multi-stage Motion Laboratory

Two sequential constant-acceleration stages. Velocity at the end of stage 1 becomes the initial velocity for stage 2.

Controls
What to notice
Never average the two accelerations blindly. Match velocity at the junction, then apply SUVAT in each piece.
  • Stage boundary shown as dashed line on the s–t sketch.
  • Signs of a₁ and a₂ can differ (speed up then slow down).

Theory & formulas

Stage 1 (from rest here)

v₁ = a₁ t₁ · s₁ = ½ a₁ t₁²

Stage 2

s₂ = v₁ t₂ + ½ a₂ t₂²

Problem tip
