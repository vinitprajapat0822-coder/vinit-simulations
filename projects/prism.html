Prism & Minimum Deviation

A triangular prism deviates light. At minimum deviation the ray inside is symmetric; n relates to A and δ_m.

Controls
What to notice
Violet is deviated more than red (dispersion). Minimum deviation is the smallest total bend as incidence varies.
  • Larger n → larger δ_m.
  • Larger A → larger δ_m.
