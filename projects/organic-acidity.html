Deep theory · exceptions · JEE traps

Core logic

Stronger HA ⇔ more stable A⁻ (in the same solvent / conditions).

Stabilising A⁻

Resonance · −I / electron-withdrawing groups · more EN atom holding charge · aromatic stabilisation of anion when relevant

JEE order (common)

Mineral / strong organic acids > carboxylic acids > phenols > water ≈ alcohols ≫ amines (as acids)
Trap: Gas-phase acidities and solvent effects can reorder some pairs — exam questions usually imply aqueous textbook trends unless stated otherwise.
