Tension & Elevator Laboratory

A mass (or elevator cabin) accelerating vertically. Tension differs from weight: T = m(g+a) with a upward positive.

Controls
What to notice
Accelerating upward → T > mg (you feel heavier). Free fall a = −g → T = 0.
  • a = 0 ⇒ T = mg
  • Atwood and elevators use the same Newton’s law idea.
