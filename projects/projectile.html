Projectile Motion Laboratory

Launch a projectile under constant gravity. Horizontal velocity stays constant; vertical motion is uniformly accelerated. Compare the dashed ideal path with the live trail and velocity vector.

Controls
What to notice
Range is maximum near 45° (flat ground, no air). Complementary angles (30° and 60°) give the same range but different flight times and max heights.
  • Gold arrow = instantaneous velocity; its vertical part shrinks, then grows downward.
  • At the top, v_y = 0 but acceleration is still g downward.
  • Change g (Moon ≈ 1.6) and watch T, R, H scale.

Theory · problem links

Components

u_x = u cosθ · u_y = u sinθ

Key results (flat ground)

T = 2 u sinθ / g
R = u² sin2θ / g
H = u² sin²θ / (2g)

Position

x = (u cosθ) t · y = (u sinθ)t − ½gt²

Problem use

For a target (x,y), eliminate t to get the trajectory equation; solve for θ or minimum u.
