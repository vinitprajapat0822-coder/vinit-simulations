PROJECTILE MOTION

Projectile Motion Laboratory

Launch over level ground. Change speed and angle; watch path, velocity components, range, max height, and time of flight — the same numbers you need in textbook / JEE problems.

Launch controls
What to notice
Horizontal motion has constant velocity. Vertical motion has constant acceleration −g. The two are independent.
  • Max range on level ground is at 45° (no air).
  • Time of flight depends only on u_y and g.
  • At the top, v_y = 0 but v_x is unchanged.
Live results (use these in numericals)
Theory & formulas

Setup

Projectile launched from origin with speed u at angle θ. Air resistance neglected.

Components

u_x = u cosθ (constant)
u_y = u sinθ (changes under gravity)

Position at time t

x = (u cosθ) t
y = (u sinθ) t − ½ g t²

Key results (lands at y = 0)

T = 2 u sinθ / g
R = u² sin(2θ) / g
H = u² sin²θ / (2g)

How to use for problems

  • Set the same u and θ as your question; read R, H, T from the panel.
  • Compare 30° and 60° — same range, different height and time.
  • Watch vectors: only the vertical component shrinks, stops, then grows downward.
