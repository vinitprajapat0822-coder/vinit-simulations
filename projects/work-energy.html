WORK & ENERGY

Work–Energy Laboratory

Work by a constant force W = Fs cosθ. Work–energy theorem: net work equals change in kinetic energy.

Controls
What to notice
Only the component of force along the displacement does work. Perpendicular force → W = 0.
  • θ = 90° → no work, no change in speed from that force.
  • Work–energy: W_net = ΔKE.

Problem tip

Choose a system so that unknown forces do no work when possible (e.g. pure rolling, fixed pivot).
