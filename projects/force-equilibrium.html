VECTOR FORCES

Resultant of Two Forces

Two forces at an angle. Resultant magnitude from the law of cosines; components make the calculation straightforward.

Controls
What to notice
Equilibrium of a particle needs the vector sum of all forces to be zero — add a third force equal to −R.
  • θ = 0° → forces add (maximum R).
  • θ = 180° → forces subtract.

Theory & formulas

Magnitude

R = √(F₁² + F₂² + 2 F₁ F₂ cosθ)

Components

R_x = F₁ + F₂ cosθ · R_y = F₂ sinθ

Problem tip

Resolve into perpendicular components, then recombine — fewer errors than pure geometry.
