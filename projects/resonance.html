RESONANCE

Resonance Laboratory

A driven oscillator responds most strongly when the drive frequency is near the natural frequency ω₀ — resonance.

Controls
What to notice
Near resonance amplitude grows large (limited by damping in real systems). Far from resonance the response is weak.
  • Pushing a swing at the right tempo builds height.
  • TAC / radio tuning selects one frequency via resonance.

Theory & formulas

Idea

Amplitude peaks when ω_drive ≈ ω₀

Problem tip

With damping, the peak shifts slightly and the height is finite: quality factor Q describes sharpness.
