Friction Laboratory

Horizontal surface: static friction matches applied force up to μN. Beyond that the block accelerates with kinetic friction μN opposite velocity.

Controls
What to notice
Until F exceeds f_max, friction equals F and net force is zero — the block does not start moving.
  • f_max = μN = μmg on level ground.
  • Once sliding, a = (F − μ_k N)/m (here μ_k ≈ μ).
