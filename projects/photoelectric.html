PHOTOELECTRIC

Photoelectric Effect Laboratory

Photons hit a metal. If hf > φ, electrons are emitted with K_max = hf − φ. Intensity changes number of electrons, not K_max.

Controls
What to notice
Below threshold frequency, even a very bright light emits no electrons. Above threshold, brighter light means more electrons per second — same K_max.
  • Stopping potential: eV₀ = K_max.
  • This is the experiment that supports the photon picture of light.
