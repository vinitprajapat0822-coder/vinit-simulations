MAGNETIC FORCE

Magnetic Force on a Wire

Force on a straight current-carrying wire: F = ILB sinθ. Direction from the right-hand rule.

Controls
What to notice
Maximum force when current is perpendicular to B. Zero when parallel (θ = 0).
  • Right-hand rule: fingers along I, bend toward B, thumb gives F on + current.
  • For a charge: F = qvB sinθ.

Theory & formulas

Wire

F = I L B sinθ

Point charge

F = q v B sinθ

Problem tip

θ is the angle between the direction of I (or v) and B.
