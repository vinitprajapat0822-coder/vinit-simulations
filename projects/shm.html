SHM

Simple Harmonic Motion Laboratory

Mass–spring oscillator. Displacement is sinusoidal; energy continuously swaps between kinetic and spring potential.

Controls
What to notice
Period does not depend on amplitude for ideal SHM. Larger k or smaller m → faster oscillation.
  • At extremes: max PE, zero KE, zero speed.
  • At mean position: max speed and KE, zero spring PE (if PE zero at equilibrium).

Theory · problem links

Motion

x = A cos(ωt + φ) · ω = √(k/m)

Period

T = 2π √(m/k)

Energy

E = ½ k A² = KE + PE

Problem tip

For vertical springs, equilibrium already balances mg; SHM is about the displacement from that equilibrium.
