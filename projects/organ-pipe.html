ORGAN PIPES

Organ Pipe Laboratory

Open and closed pipes support different harmonics. Open: all integer harmonics. Closed: odd harmonics only.

Controls
What to notice
Open end ≈ displacement antinode. Closed end ≈ displacement node.
  • Open fundamental: λ = 2L.
  • Closed fundamental: λ = 4L (half the open frequency for same L).
