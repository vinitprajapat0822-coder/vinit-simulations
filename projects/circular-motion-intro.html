What to notice
Even at constant speed, there is acceleration because direction changes. Net force toward centre is required.
  • Double v → 4× a_c.
  • Double r → half a_c (same v).

Theory & formulas

Centripetal

a_c = v²/r = ω²r
F_c = m v²/r
T = 2πr/v

Problem tip

F_c is not a new force type — it is the net force toward the centre (tension, gravity, friction, etc.).
