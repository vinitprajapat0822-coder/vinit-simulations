MAGNETOSTATICS

Magnetic Field of a Straight Wire

A long straight current produces circular magnetic field lines. Magnitude falls as 1/r.

Controls
What to notice
Right-hand rule: thumb along current, fingers curl in the direction of B.
  • Double I → double B.
  • Double r → half B.
Live results
Theory & formulas

Infinite straight wire

B = μ₀ I / (2π r)
μ₀/2π = 2 × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A

Problem tip

Direction is separate from the scalar formula — always state it with the right-hand rule.
