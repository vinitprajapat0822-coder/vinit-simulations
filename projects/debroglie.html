de Broglie Wavelength

Every moving particle has a wavelength: λ = h / (mv). Change mass and speed — watch λ update. This is why electrons show interference but cricket balls do not.

Core idea
λ = h / p = h / (m v)
h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s (Planck constant)
Small m or large v → larger λ → wave nature easier to see
Large m (ball, human) → λ ~ 10⁻³⁴ m → wave nature invisible
