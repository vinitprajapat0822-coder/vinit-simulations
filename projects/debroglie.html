MATTER WAVES

de Broglie Wavelength Laboratory

Any particle with momentum p has wavelength λ = h/p. Electrons show measurable wavelengths; macroscopic objects have absurdly small λ.

Controls
What to notice
Electron diffraction experiments confirmed matter waves. Larger mass or speed → smaller λ.
  • λ_electron can be comparable to atomic spacings.
  • A cricket ball’s λ is ~10⁻³⁴ m — not observable as a wave.

Theory & formulas

de Broglie

λ = h / p = h / (mv)

With kinetic energy

λ = h / √(2mK)

Problem tip

For electrons accelerated by V volts: K = eV, then use λ = h/√(2m eV).
