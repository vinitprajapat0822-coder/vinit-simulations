Beats Laboratory

Two close frequencies produce a slow amplitude modulation — the beat frequency is |f₁ − f₂|.

Controls
What to notice
You hear the average pitch, but loudness waxes and wanes at f_beat.
  • Closer frequencies → slower beats.
  • Used to tune instruments against a reference tone.

Theory & formulas

Beat frequency

f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|

Superposition

y = A[sin(2πf₁t) + sin(2πf₂t)]

Problem tip

If a tuning fork of 512 Hz produces 4 beats/s with a string, the string is 508 or 516 Hz — need another clue to decide.
