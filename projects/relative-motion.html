Relative Velocity Laboratory

Two objects move on a line. Relative velocity of A w.r.t. B is v_A − v_B. Sign tells who is pulling ahead.

Controls
What to notice
If both move the same way at the same speed, relative velocity is zero — they stay a fixed distance apart.
  • Closing speed when approaching head-on is |v_A| + |v_B|.
  • Use relative velocity for overtaking and meeting problems.

Theory & formulas

1D relative velocity

v_{AB} = v_A − v_B

Position

x_A − x_B changes at rate v_{AB}

Problem tip

Pick a positive direction once; every velocity is signed.
