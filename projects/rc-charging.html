RC CIRCUIT

RC Charging Laboratory

A capacitor charges through a resistor toward the battery voltage. Time constant τ = RC sets how fast V_C approaches V.

Controls
What to notice
After one τ, V_C ≈ 63% of V. After ~5τ the capacitor is essentially fully charged.
  • Current starts at V/R and falls to zero.
  • Larger R or C → slower charging.

Theory & formulas

Charging

V_C = V(1 − e^{−t/RC})
I = (V/R) e^{−t/RC}
τ = RC

Problem tip

For discharge: V_C = V₀ e^{−t/RC}.
