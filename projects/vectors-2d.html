2D Vector Addition Laboratory

Add two plane vectors tip-to-tail. Read the magnitude and direction of the resultant — the foundation for forces, velocities, and displacement problems.

What to notice
Tip-to-tail: place the tail of B at the tip of A. The resultant R runs from the start of A to the tip of B.
  • Components: R_x = A + B cosθ, R_y = B sinθ (A along x).
  • |R|² = A² + B² + 2AB cosθ.

Theory & formulas

Magnitude

|R| = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ)

Direction from A

tanφ = (B sinθ) / (A + B cosθ)

Problem tip

Resolve into perpendicular components, add components, then rebuild the magnitude. This lab shows both the geometry and the numbers.
