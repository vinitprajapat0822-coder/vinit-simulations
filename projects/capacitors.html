What to notice
Parallel: same voltage, charges add. Series: same charge, voltages add.
  • Parallel C_eq is larger than either capacitor.
  • Series C_eq is smaller than the smaller capacitor.

Theory & formulas

Parallel

C_eq = C₁ + C₂

Series

1/C_eq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂

Energy

U = ½ C_eq V²
