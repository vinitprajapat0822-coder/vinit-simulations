Inclined Plane with Friction

A block on a rough incline. See when friction holds the block and when it slides — and the acceleration down the plane.

Controls
What to notice
Component of weight down the plane is mg sinθ. Friction up the plane is at most μN = μ mg cosθ.
  • Slides if tanθ > μ (for the simple μ_s = μ model).
  • Acceleration when sliding: g(sinθ − μ cosθ).

Problem tip
