Deep theory · exceptions · JEE traps

Definition (exam wording)

Delocalisation of σ electrons of a C–H bond of an alkyl group directly attached to an electron-deficient centre / unsaturated system.

Classic structure count

tert-butyl cation: 9 hyperconjugative structures in many JEE texts.

Also stabilises

Alkenes (more substituted often more stable), radicals — with the same comparative logic.
Trap: Allylic/benzylic carbocations can beat 3° alkyl because of resonance — do not apply the alkyl-only order blindly.

Limits

Counting structures is a bookkeeping model; real energetics depend on geometry and full electronic structure.
