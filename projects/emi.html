ELECTROMAGNETIC INDUCTION

Motional EMF Laboratory

A conducting rod slides on rails in a uniform magnetic field. The induced emf is Bℓv for this standard geometry.

Controls
What to notice
Flux through the loop changes because the area is changing. Faraday: ε = dΦ/dt leads to ε = Bℓv.
  • Direction of induced current: Lenz’s law (oppose the change in flux).
  • Faster rod or stronger B → larger emf.

Theory & formulas

Motional emf (rod on rails)

ε = B ℓ v
(B perpendicular to the plane, v perpendicular to the rod)

Faraday

ε = − dΦ_B / dt · Φ_B = B · A

Problem tip

Check that B, ℓ, and v are mutually oriented as in the standard derivation before using ε = Bℓv.
