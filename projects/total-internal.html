TIR

Total Internal Reflection

When light goes denser → rarer, beyond the critical angle all light reflects. Basis of optical fibres.

Controls
What to notice
Critical angle: sin i_c = n₂/n₁ (n₁ > n₂). For i > i_c → TIR.
  • Gold ray = incident, purple = reflected, cyan = refracted (if any).
  • Optical fibre: core higher n than cladding.
