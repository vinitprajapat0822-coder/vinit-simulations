Energy in SHM Laboratory

In ideal SHM, total mechanical energy is constant. Watch PE and KE trade places as the mass oscillates.

Controls
What to notice
At maximum displacement, PE is maximum and KE is zero. At equilibrium, KE is maximum and PE is zero (if U = 0 at equilibrium).
  • E = ½kA² does not depend on m.
  • Doubling A multiplies energy by 4.

Theory & formulas

Energy in SHM

E = ½kA² = constant
U(x) = ½kx²
KE = E − U

Problem tip

If asked for speed at displacement x: ½mv² = ½k(A² − x²).
