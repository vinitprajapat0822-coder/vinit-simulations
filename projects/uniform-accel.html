Uniform Acceleration Laboratory

Constant acceleration along a straight line. Connect the SUVAT equations to a moving object and its velocity–time graph (area = displacement).

Controls
What to notice
On a v–t graph, slope is acceleration and area under the curve is displacement.
  • If a and u have opposite signs, the object may stop and reverse.
  • s = ut + ½at² still holds with signed quantities.

Theory & formulas

SUVAT (constant a)

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as
s = (u+v)t/2

Problem tip

Pick a positive direction once. Then every quantity in the formula is a signed number — this lab uses that convention.
