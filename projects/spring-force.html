HOOKE'S LAW

Hooke's Law & Mass–Spring

A mass on a spring oscillates. Force is −kx; energy sloshes between spring PE and KE while total E stays constant (ideal case).

Controls
What to notice
Restoring force always points toward equilibrium: F = −kx.
  • Period T = 2π √(m/k) — independent of amplitude for ideal Hooke’s law.
  • Max PE at the ends; max KE at the centre.

Force & energy

U = ½kx²
E = ½kA² (mechanical, no damping)
