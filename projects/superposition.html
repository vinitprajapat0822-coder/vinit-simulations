Wave Superposition Laboratory

Two harmonic waves of the same frequency add. Resultant amplitude depends on the phase difference.

What to notice
Δφ = 0° → constructive (amplitudes add). Δφ = 180° → destructive (amplitudes subtract).
  • Green curve = sum of the two faint waves.
  • Beats arise when frequencies differ slightly.
