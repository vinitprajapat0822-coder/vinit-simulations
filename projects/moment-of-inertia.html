For a point mass, I = mr² about the axis. Mass farther from the axis is harder to angularly accelerate.

What to notice
I plays the role of mass in τ = Iα. Same mass closer to the axis → smaller I → larger α for same torque.
  • Double r → 4× I.
  • Extended bodies: integrate r² dm (standard results for rod, disc, sphere).
