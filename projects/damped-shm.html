DAMPED SHM

Damped Oscillations Laboratory

With friction or air resistance, amplitude decays roughly as e^{−γt}. Energy is gradually lost from the oscillator.

Controls
What to notice
Light damping: many oscillations before dying out. Heavy damping: may return to equilibrium without oscillating.
  • Shock absorbers are designed damping.
  • Ideal SHM is the γ → 0 limit.
