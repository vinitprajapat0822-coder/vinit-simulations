Deep theory · exceptions · JEE traps

Definition (JEE style)

Permanent polarity shift transmitted along a chain through σ bonds due to electronegativity / electron-donating differences.

Typical −I ranking (teaching)

Often: NO₂ > F > Cl > Br (tables can vary slightly by source — learn the idea, not only memorised numbers).

Typical +I

Alkyl groups are weakly +I; branched alkyl can be discussed as stronger donors in many textbooks.

Where it matters

Acid strength of halogenated acids, carbocation/carbanion stability arguments, directing effects when combined with resonance.
Trap: Inductive ≠ resonance. −I of Cl and +R of Cl on benzene are different effects operating together.

Limits of this lab

Bars are a pedagogical scale, not Mulliken charges from quantum software.
