Inductive Effect Explorer

Teaching model of +I / −I groups on a carbon chain. Strength falls off with distance — qualitative, not a computed charge map.

Controls
What to notice
−I groups (NO₂, F, Cl) withdraw electron density through σ bonds. +I alkyl groups donate weakly. Effect decreases sharply down the chain.
  • Compare NO₂ on C1 vs C3.
  • CH₃ is weakly +I relative to H.

Theory · limits

Idea

Inductive effect operates through sigma bonds and is distance-dependent.

JEE ranking (common)

−I strength often taught as: NO₂ > F > Cl (context-dependent tables exist).

Limits
